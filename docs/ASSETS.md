# Asset provenance

- All three bundled notes are original synthetic writing for this project: **A slower kind of morning**, **Things worth making**, and **For the next little trip**. They contain no real personal records, private source, copied application material, or secret credentials.
- `fixtures/sample-state.json` is generated from `sample()` in `src/domain/model.ts`. It is a plain version-1 envelope with empty outboxes, drafts, receipts, and ledger. Its IDs are synthetic example identifiers.
- The book mark, laptop/pocket outlines, pinned paper decoration, dividers, and notebook rules are original CSS geometry. Text arrows and line symbols are typographic characters; no downloaded icon pack or emoji artwork is used.
- Typography uses local/system sans-serif and Georgia/Times serif stacks. The project does not bundle or request font files.
- `docs/screenshots/desktop.png` and `docs/screenshots/mobile.png` are actual Chromium captures of the running application at 1440 × 1050 and 390 × 844 viewport sizes. Full-page images are taller than their viewports. Their displayed Local work tray, conflict and ledger come from real saved/synchronized synthetic actions in `tests/e2e/notebook.spec.ts`.
- No stock photography, generated bitmap illustration, private product assets, third-party tracking images, or remote media are included.

Original code, text, CSS decoration, fixtures, and screenshots are covered by this repository's MIT license. Dependency licenses remain with their respective packages and are represented by the installed packages and lockfile.
